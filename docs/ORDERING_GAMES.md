# Ordering games

`order` retains stable-ID `items` and an explicit `correctOrder` permutation. Optional `prompt` supplies the constraint and `variant` identifies sentence, story, procedure or chronology. Four debug levels demonstrate these modes. The sentence prompt explicitly specifies a verb first to avoid valid alternative Arabic word orders. Story and procedure instructions scope answers to the supplied task.

All fixtures are original drafted text, debug only and unapproved. Historical, religious and poetry content remains deferred pending authoritative sourcing and appropriate review. Local solving does not grant publication approval or competition eligibility.
